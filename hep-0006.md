## Why Warm Lights
Although a bit more expensive, warm lights create cozier atmospheres for work and play. They look better on photos and videos. Cool lights create hospital like atmosphere, yucks.

3000K was the easiest to find warm lighting option. Matches our trunklinea task lights in color which are installed at the maker corner.

If you are interested in reading more about color temperature you can check out [the wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_temperature).
