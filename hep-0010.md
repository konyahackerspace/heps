## Introduction

This HEP proposes purchasing ergonomic office chairs for the hackerspace to improve comfort and productivity during long working sessions, and to reach a sufficient number of chairs for hosting workshops.

Current seating options are not ideal for prolonged use, especially for:
- programming sessions
- workshops and events
